# Trove

A personal library for books, games, travel and workouts that works offline on the phone. Its gaming part is also a buying guide: it tracks prices for your games, says which of your devices to play each one on, and recommends games on sale.

## Language

### Your games

**Game**:
A video game in your Trove library, whatever its status.
_Avoid_: Title, entry, item

**Status**:
Where a game stands for you: wishlist, owned, played or dropped. Each status is a tab on the Games page.
_Avoid_: State, category, list

**Device**:
One of the three things you play on: the PC (the Dell G15 laptop, running Linux), the PS5 and the Switch 2. PS4 games count as PS5 games and Switch games as Switch 2 games, since they play on them.
_Avoid_: Platform (that's IGDB's word for consoles in general), system

**Match**:
The IGDB game a Trove game is linked to, which gives it its proper name, cover, studio and tags. A match is automatic, picked by you, or waiting for you to pick one when the search was ambiguous.
_Avoid_: Link, mapping, identity

**Filled-in value**:
A cover or studio the worker took from a game's match because the game had none. It's replaced if you change the match; a value you set yourself never is.
_Avoid_: Default, auto-cover

### Prices

**Store**:
Where a game is bought: Steam (India, rupees), the Nintendo eShop (US, dollars) or the PS Store. Only Steam and the eShop are priced; the PS Store is a link.
_Avoid_: Shop, marketplace

**Listing**:
One purchasable version of a game in one store, such as its Switch 2 Edition on the eShop.
_Avoid_: Product, SKU, offer

**Edition**:
Which Switch generation an eShop listing is for (Switch or Switch 2), when a game has both.
_Avoid_: Version, variant

**Price run**:
A stretch of time a listing held one price. A listing's price history is its runs, oldest first.
_Avoid_: Quote (one observation of a price), price point, snapshot

**Target price**:
The price in rupees you'd pay for a wishlisted game. Falling to it is a reason for a sale alert.
_Avoid_: Budget, threshold, limit

**Sale alert**:
A notification that a wishlisted game's price became worth knowing about: a sale started or got deeper, it reached your target price, or it's the lowest price seen. Each sale alerts once.
_Avoid_: Price alert, deal notification

**Deal**:
A wishlisted game that's on sale right now, shown in the Deals tab.
_Avoid_: Discount, offer, sale (the store's event, not your game)

### Advice

**Device verdict**:
Which of your devices to play a game on: each device it's released for gets a rating (great, good, playable or avoid) with reasons. A verdict made while some evidence was unreachable is incomplete and gets redone.
_Avoid_: Recommendation, compatibility, fit

**Pick**:
A game not in your library that's on sale and that Trove thinks you'd enjoy, one of five chosen each Sunday. You can add it to your wishlist, mark it owned, or turn it down.
_Avoid_: Recommendation, suggestion

**Taste profile**:
What your library says you like and dislike, from your ratings, statuses and the picks you turned down. Picks are scored against it.
_Avoid_: Preferences, interests
